Scope

Hermes Gmail is a personal-use integration operated by Yao-Ting Huang for their own Gmail account. It is not offered to other users.

Owner review

Hermes Gmail proposes summaries and replies. The owner checks every proposal. It takes no automatic actions on the mailbox. Hermes Gmail sends a message or reply only after the owner approves the exact recipients and content of that message. Mail monitoring and triage are read-only and never send mail.

The integration depends on Google, the configured AI provider and Telegram, and is subject to their availability and terms. There is no guarantee of continuous or permanent access.
